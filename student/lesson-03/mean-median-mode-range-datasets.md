# Mean, Median, Mode and Range Practice

For each dataset below, work out the **mean**, **median**, **mode**, and **range**.
Show your steps, not just the final number.

> Quick reminders
> - **Mean**: add up all the values, then divide by how many there are.
> - **Median**: sort the values and pick the middle one. If there's an even count, take the average of the two in the middle.
> - **Mode**: the value that shows up the most. Sometimes there's none, sometimes there's more than one.
> - **Range**: biggest value minus smallest value.

### How each one works out

- **#1:** 14 divided by 7 is 2. Sorted: 0 1 2 2 2 3 4, so the middle is 2. The number 2 shows up 3 times, so that's the mode. Range is 4 minus 0, which is 4.
- **#2:** 30 divided by 6 is 5. Sorted: 1 2 4 7 7 9. The two middle numbers are 4 and 7, and their average is 5.5. Mode is 7. Range is 9 minus 1, which is 8.
- **#3:** 150 divided by 5 is 30. Sorted: 15 20 30 35 50, so the middle is 30. Nothing repeats, so there's no mode. Range is 50 minus 15, which is 35.
- **#4:** 60 divided by 8 is 7.5. Sorted: 6 6 6 7 8 8 9 10. The two middle numbers are 7 and 8, average 7.5. Mode is 6, which shows up 3 times. Range is 10 minus 6, which is 4.
- **#5:** 146 divided by 7 is about 20.9. Sorted: 18 19 21 21 21 22 24, middle is 21. Mode is 21, which shows up 3 times. Range is 24 minus 18, which is 6.
- **#6:** 16 divided by 10 is 1.6. Sorted: 0 0 1 1 1 2 2 2 3 4. The two middle numbers are 1 and 2, average 1.5. Both 1 and 2 show up 3 times, so there are two modes. Range is 4 minus 0, which is 4.
- **#7:** 6.75 divided by 6 is 1.125. Sorted: 0.75 0.75 1.00 1.25 1.50 1.50. The two middle numbers are 1.00 and 1.25, average 1.125. Both 0.75 and 1.50 show up twice, so there are two modes. Range is 1.50 minus 0.75, which is 0.75.
- **#8:** 220 divided by 5 is 44. Sorted: 38 38 42 47 55, middle is 42. Mode is 38. Range is 55 minus 38, which is 17.
